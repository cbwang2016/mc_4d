The renderer uses the original pixels with nearest-neighbor sampling. World grass
and oak leaves use the original grasscolor.png/foliagecolor.png lookup tables,
with climate sampled from the 4D world's temperature and moisture fields.
Spruce and birch retain the original fixed species colors; Fast graphics uses
the original opaque leaf tiles. Tints and lighting follow the original encoded
RGB multiplication before converting to the renderer's linear output.
Water, lava, and fire animations use the original Beta texture-effect equations.
The 4D adaptation changes projection and climate coordinates, not source PNGs.

The first-person arm uses the original mob/char.png skin. Held blocks use
qa/cn inventory dispatch, bounds, and face tiles rather than world-context
biome textures; sprites retain the original 1/16-block extrusion and pose.
models.json and held-blocks.json record the verified client model/held-block
observations; scripts/reference contains the reflection extractors and the
corresponding scripts/generate_* tools reproduce the Rust/WGSL tables.
